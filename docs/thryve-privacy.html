Thryve Privacy Policy

App: com.bling.thryve
Developer: Bling
Updated: October 27, 2026

Privacy contact: jlenteria98@gmail.com.

Your goals and local data

Your name, big dream, anchor, profile photo, goals, milestones, logged earnings, daily practices, Wall items, weekly reviews, activity history, reminder time and appearance choice are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. Thryve has no accounts, ads, developer-run server or analytics service. Android cloud backup is disabled for this data.

Photos

Photo-library access is used only when you choose a profile photo, anchor image or Wall image. The picked photo is resized and copied into the app’s private folder. Photos are never uploaded by Thryve, and replaced or removed photos are deleted from that folder.

Image links

If you add a Wall image by pasting a web link, Thryve downloads that image directly from the website you linked. That website can see standard request details such as your IP address and handles them under its own policy. Thryve sends nothing else with the request.

Notifications

The optional daily reminder is scheduled on your device and may include your name and anchor, which can appear on your lock screen depending on your system settings. No notification content passes through a server.

Support

Optional support payments take place through your chosen wallet or bank app. Thryve does not receive payment credentials or grant features for contributions.

Retention and deletion

Your data remains until you change or delete it. Activity history older than 400 days is removed automatically. Reset all local data in Settings, clearing app storage, or uninstalling Thryve deletes all app records and stored photos. Payment records remain with their recipients.

Your choices and contact

You may revoke photo or notification permissions in system settings, turn off the daily reminder, delete any goal or Wall item, and choose whether to contribute. Revoking permissions may prevent picking photos or receiving reminders. Thryve has no online account to delete.

For privacy questions, contact jlenteria98@gmail.com. Material changes will be reflected in the policy available in Settings and on the public policy page.
