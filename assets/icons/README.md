# Thryve logo

A growing lowercase **t**: two ascending leaves and a gently curved stem.
The open spaces keep the symbol legible at small sizes, while the upward leaf
represents intentional progress.

## Assets

- `thryve_icon.svg` / `thryve_icon.png`: rounded emerald app-icon artwork, 1024 × 1024.
- `thryve_mark.svg` / `thryve_mark.png`: standalone emerald symbol, transparent background.
- `thryve_wordmark.svg` / `thryve_wordmark.png`: horizontal logo, transparent background.

The SVG wordmark uses Geist SemiBold, bundled in `assets/fonts/Geist`.
The PNG wordmark has its lettering rendered and needs no installed fonts.

## Palette

| Color | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emerald | `#006D41` | Primary mark |
| Deep emerald | `#003920` | Wordmark |
| Fresh mint | `#9AF0BC` | Icon's upward leaf |
| Soft ivory | `#F8FAF9` | Icon's stem and left leaf |

Keep clear space of at least one stem-width around the visible symbol.
The supplied files are brand artwork; platform launcher icons can be generated
from these separately.
